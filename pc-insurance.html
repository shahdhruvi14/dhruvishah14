What did I build? P&C Insurance - Policyholder Experience A centralized self-service portal designed to give policyholders a clear view of their insurance relationship in one place. The experience brings together active policies, policy details, documents, billing, premiums, coverage periods, and assigned agent information, while introducing a new digital claims experience. The portal was designed to align with Guidewire's policy, claims, and billing workflows, covering Commercial Property, General Liability, Workers' Compensation, Business Auto, and more.
Problem Policyholders had access to their policy information through an existing portal, but the experience needed to be redesigned to make complex insurance information easier to understand and navigate. At the same time, claims were handled offline or through separate online channels, creating a disconnected experience and making it harder for policyholders to track their claims journey.
Goal The goal was to create a clear, centralized policyholder experience that makes insurance information easier to find, understand, and manage. The redesign focused on simplifying policy management while introducing a seamless digital claims journey, aligned with Guidewire workflows and established insurance UX patterns.

How did I get there? Process to Achieve the Goal
UX Audit & UX Opportunities
Understanding what policyholders see, what they miss, and what they need next. This revealed a key UX gap: the experience was information-rich but action-light. The redesign opportunity was to move beyond simply displaying policy data and help users quickly understand what needs attention, what is due, and what to do next.
•Limited dashboard visibility - only active policies surfaced prominently after login.
•Deep navigation - important information required users to navigate through the menu.
•View-only experience - policy details were available, but actionable next steps were unclear.
•Status visibility - limited indication of where a policy or claim currently stood.
•Action prioritization - due, pending, and important actions were not clearly surfaced.
•Next-step guidance - users were not always told what they needed to do next.
•Information vs. action - the portal showed what exists but did not sufficiently communicate what needs attention.
The Existing Experience
From UX Insights to Key UI Decisions
01 Critical Actions Are Hoisted, Not Buried
Decision The pending-claim banner is pulled out of the scrollable policy list and placed prominently at the top with the exact policy number and required action.
UX Impact Time-sensitive, blocking tasks are seen first rather than competing with routine policy information.
02 The Right Column Adapts to Each Policy's State
Decision The policy overview dynamically surfaces information based on the policy's current state — premium due, renewal approaching, or simply active.
UX Impact Users see the most relevant status, action, or information for each policy without adding unnecessary complexity.
03 High-Stakes Actions Have Redundant Entry Points
Decision “Report a Claim” is reachable from the global header, individual policy actions, and the critical claim banner.
UX Impact Multiple entry points let users reach a high-priority action quickly, especially during stressful moments.
Report a Claim - global entry point
04 The Agent Is a Persistent Human Presence
Decision Agent details — name, address, call and email actions — stay anchored in the lower-left corner of the workspace.
UX Impact A consistent point of human assistance is available whenever users need support throughout their insurance journey.
05 Progressive Disclosure Keeps the Interface Focused
Decision Less frequently needed actions and deeper policy details are tucked behind a contextual actions menu.
UX Impact The primary interface stays clean and scannable while everything remains accessible when needed.
06 The Workspace Adapts to the User's Task
Decision The side navigation collapses to an icon rail, letting users reclaim horizontal space while reviewing detailed information.
UX Impact A more focused reading experience, without permanently removing navigation.
