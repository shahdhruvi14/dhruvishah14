What did I Build Product Testing Tool
This platform focused on testing and collecting feedback on hardware products across various categories, including apparel, footwear, and home goods. The platform connects real testers with brands to evaluate products in real-world conditions, providing actionable insights that can guide product improvements and innovation. Validate Product with Valuable Customer Feedback On A Single-Platform Product Testing Solution. Hence, the core mission is: Real People. Real Testing. Real Feedback.
Problem The existing platform made it difficult for brands to efficiently manage product tests and make sense of the large volume of tester feedback. Key testing activities and insights were spread across different areas, creating a fragmented experience for users. Testers also needed a simpler and more intuitive way to provide feedback throughout the testing process.
Goal The goal was to create a more streamlined and intuitive testing experience for both brands and testers. The redesign focused on simplifying test management, improving feedback collection, and making insights easier to understand and act upon. The experience was designed to help brands make faster, more informed product decisions based on real-world feedback.

Designing the Solution
From Question Builder to Survey Experience
01
Challenge The legacy experience used a persistent side-by-side preview that consumed significant screen space, even though analysts were primarily focused on building and managing questions.
Approach Apply the WYSIWYG principle directly to the question-building experience, eliminating the need for a separate preview panel.
Solution & UX Impact Removing the redundant preview created more working space for question management while keeping the creation experience representative of the final tester experience. This allowed important actions and survey structure to remain visible without overwhelming the workspace.

02
Challenge Creating and managing parent-child questions required additional effort and made conditional survey structures harder to understand.
Approach Use direct manipulation through drag-and-drop to make hierarchy creation part of the natural question-ordering interaction.
Solution & UX Impact Analysts can quickly reorder questions or nest them under a parent question, making complex conditional flows easier to build, understand, and maintain.

03
Challenge Showing multiple expanded questions created unnecessary visual density and required analysts to scroll through long forms to focus on a specific question.
Approach Use progressive disclosure — keep only the active question expanded while collapsing completed or inactive questions.
Solution & UX Impact The interface keeps the analyst focused on one question at a time, reducing cognitive load and creating a more structured, task-oriented workflow.

Community - Finding the Right Testers
With a community of 95,000+ testers, analysts need to quickly identify the right participants for each product test. The redesigned experience makes complex filtering intuitive and easy to navigate, allowing analysts to narrow the tester pool based on product-specific criteria. Once the right audience is identified, analysts can collapse the filter pane to create more space for reviewing and selecting testers, keeping the focus on the final selection.

Product Tests — Managing Tests at a Glance
As the primary workspace for analysts, the Product Tests screen brings all active and completed tests into one place. With key details such as test name, category, dates, status, owners, creators, and ratings presented together, analysts can quickly scan test progress and identify what needs attention. Contextual actions are kept easily accessible for each test, allowing analysts to manage their tests efficiently without unnecessary navigation.
